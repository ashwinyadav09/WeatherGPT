# NDMA National Guidelines: Heatwave Safety

## Do's During Extreme Heat
- Drink sufficient water and stay hydrated even if not thirsty. Carry drinking water when traveling.
- Use Oral Rehydration Solution (ORS), homemade drinks like lassi, torani (rice water), lemon water, and buttermilk to stay hydrated.
- Wear lightweight, light-coloured, loose, and porous cotton clothes. Cover your head using a cloth, hat, or umbrella.
- Keep animals in shade and provide them plenty of drinking water.
- If you feel faint, dizzy, or ill, see a doctor immediately or use a damp cloth on your head and neck.
- In case of heat stroke, call emergency helpline 112 or disaster helpline 1077.

## Don'ts During Extreme Heat
- Do not leave children or pets inside parked locked vehicles under direct sunlight.
- Avoid going out in direct sun, especially between 12:00 noon and 3:00 PM.
- Avoid strenuous activities during peak afternoon hours.
- Avoid alcohol, tea, coffee, and carbonated soft drinks, which dehydrate the body.
- Avoid high-protein, spicy, stale, or oily food.
- Key terms: दोपहर की धूप, क्या न करें, शराब, parked vehicles, children, pets, direct sun, peak heat.

## Vulnerable Groups Protection
- Infants, young children, pregnant women, outdoor labourers, and elderly persons need special care and monitoring.
- Cool indoor spaces with curtains, fans, and wet mats; keep windows closed during hot daytime hours and open at night.

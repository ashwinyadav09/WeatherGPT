# NDMA National Guidelines: Cold Wave Safety

## Do's During Cold Wave (Source: IMD-NDMA Guidelines, Page 1)
- Have adequate winter clothing. Multiple layers of loose-fitting, lightweight clothing are more effective than one thick layer.
- Have emergency supplies, warm blankets, and medical necessities ready.
- Stay indoors as much as possible; minimise travel to prevent exposure to severe cold wind.
- Keep dry; if clothes get wet, change quickly to prevent loss of body heat.
- Prefer mittens over gloves; mittens provide better warmth and insulation from cold.
- Keep head, neck, hands, and feet warmly covered with caps, mufflers, gloves, and socks.
- Drink hot drinks regularly to maintain body temperature.
- Ensure adequate ventilation and fresh airflow when using charcoal angithi, coal braziers, or kerosene heaters indoors to prevent carbon monoxide poisoning.

## Don'ts, Hypothermia and Frostbite Treatment (Source: IMD-NDMA Guidelines, Page 1-2)
- Do not drink alcohol in extreme cold; alcohol dilates blood vessels and reduces core body temperature.
- Do not ignore shivering; shivering is the first warning sign that the body is losing heat and a signal to return indoors.
- Watch out for symptoms of frostbite: numbness, white or pale appearance on fingers, toes, ear lobes, and tip of the nose.
- Put areas affected by frostbite in warm (not hot) water; do not massage or rub frostbitten areas vigorously, as this causes tissue damage.
- Hypothermia first aid: move person to a warm room, change wet clothes, rewarm body with dry blankets and warm drinks (never alcohol).
- Seek medical attention immediately if body temperature drops or disorientation occurs.

## Vulnerable Groups and Winter Shelter (Source: IMD-NDMA Guidelines, Page 1 & 3)
- Special protection for vulnerable populations: newborn infants, elderly individuals, chronically ill persons, and homeless citizens.
- Maintain comfortable indoor room temperature and protect children and elderly from drafts.
- District administrations provide night shelters (Rain Baseras) equipped with blankets and hot water for homeless persons during severe cold wave.
- Emergency assistance and shelter helplines: call National Emergency helpline 112 or Disaster helpline 1077.
- Domestic cattle and pets protection: keep animals inside sheds at night, provide dry bedding, protect from cold winds, and increase protein/mineral rations.

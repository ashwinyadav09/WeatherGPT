# NDMA National Guidelines: Cold Wave Safety

## Do's During Cold Wave
- Wear multiple layers of loose-fitting, lightweight, warm clothing rather than one thick layer.
- Keep your head, neck, hands, and feet covered warmly with caps, mufflers, gloves, and woolen socks, as significant heat loss occurs from extremities.
- Eat nutritious food rich in vitamin C and warm nutritious soups to maintain body heat and immunity.
- Maintain adequate ventilation when using room heaters, coal stoves, or charcoal angithi to prevent dangerous carbon monoxide buildup.
- Provide warm shelter, dry bedding, and clean water to pets and farm animals.

## Don'ts During Cold Wave
- Do not drink alcohol, as it dilates blood vessels and lowers your core body temperature rapidly despite a false sensation of warmth.
- Avoid prolonged exposure to cold winds and damp conditions to prevent frostbite and hypothermia.
- Do not ignore shivering; persistent shivering is the first warning sign of body heat loss and hypothermia.
- Do not massage frostbitten areas with snow or rub vigorously, as this causes severe tissue damage. Seek immediate medical attention.

## Elderly and Children Care
- Keep room temperature comfortable for elderly citizens, newborn babies, and heart patients.
- Contact national emergency helpline 112 or local disaster management helpline 1077 for shelter assistance.

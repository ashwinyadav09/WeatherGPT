# Flood, Cyclone and Lightning Safety Guidelines

## Heavy Rain and Urban Flooding Do's and Don'ts
- Stay away from flooded streets, drainage channels, culverts, and downed electrical poles or hanging power lines.
- Do not drive into flooded roads; as little as 30 cm (1 foot) of moving water can float a vehicle.
- Disconnect electrical appliances and turn off the main gas and electricity switches if water starts entering your home.
- Drink boiled or chlorinated water to avoid waterborne infectious diseases following floods.

## Lightning and Thunderstorm Precautions
- Follow the 30-30 lightning safety rule: If the time between seeing lightning and hearing thunder is less than 30 seconds, seek shelter inside a sturdy building or closed metal vehicle immediately.
- Never take shelter under tall isolated trees, open metal sheds, tin roofs, or flagpoles during thunderstorms.
- If caught in an open field with no shelter nearby, crouch low on the balls of your feet with hands on knees and head tucked down; minimize contact with the ground. Do not lie flat on the ground.
- Discontinue mobile phone charging and unplug high-voltage electrical equipment during severe thunderstorms.
- National Emergency Helpline: 112. Disaster Management Control Room: 1077.

# Flood, Cyclone and Lightning Safety Guidelines

## Flood Do's and Don'ts (Source: IMD-NDMA Guidelines, Page 1-2)
- Before and during floods: ignore rumours, stay calm, and don't panic.
- Keep mobile phones charged for emergency communication.
- Turn off gas valves and electrical power mains before flood water enters premises.
- Do not drive into flooded roads; as little as 30 cm (1 foot) of moving water can float a car.
- Stay away from flooded streets, drainage channels, culverts, and downed electrical poles or hanging power lines.
- Evacuate immediately to designated high ground or cyclone/flood shelters when advised by district administration.
- Do not allow children to play in or near flood waters; water may be contaminated and carry disease.

## Cyclone and Lightning Safety Measures (Source: IMD-NDMA Guidelines, Page 1-3)
- Cyclone precautions: inspect and secure loose roof tiles or tin sheets; board or tape glass windows.
- Keep emergency kits ready: non-perishable food, potable drinking water, torches, battery radio, and first-aid supplies.
- Unhook and disconnect electrical appliances during cyclones to prevent short-circuits and electrical fires.
- Lightning safety 30/30 rule: if the time between lightning flash and thunder is less than 30 seconds, take shelter indoors; wait 30 minutes after last thunder before heading outdoors.
- If outdoor during lightning: avoid tall isolated trees, open fields, elevated ridges, and metal structures; if hair stands on neck, squat low with hands on knees.

# Coastal and Fisherfolk Marine Weather Safety Guidance

## Sea Venture Restrictions and Weather Warnings
- Fisherfolk must not venture into the sea when squally weather with wind speeds exceeding 40 to 50 km/h or rough to very rough sea conditions are forecasted.
- Fisherfolk already out at sea should return to the nearest harbor or coast immediately when an advisory or warning of deteriorating sea conditions is issued.
- Check INCOIS Ocean State Forecasts and coastal bulletins daily for significant wave height, swell surge, and high wave alerts before embarking.

## Vessel and Equipment Safeguards
- Secure small fishing boats, catamarans, trawlers, and dinghies well above the high tide line on beaches or inside protected bays.
- Keep VHF marine radio sets, distress alert transmitters (DAT), life jackets, and emergency signal flares on board and fully operational.
- Avoid operating near rocky shores, breakwaters, and harbor mouths during high swell periods.
- In marine emergencies or vessel distress, contact the Indian Coast Guard emergency helpline at 1554.

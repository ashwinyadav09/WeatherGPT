# Coastal and Fisherfolk Marine Weather Safety Guidance

## Sea Venture Restrictions and Weather Warnings (Source: IMD Marine Fishermen Warning Bulletin, Page 1)
- Fishermen are strictly advised not to venture into the sea when squally weather with wind speed reaching 45-55 kmph gusting to 65 kmph is expected.
- Sea conditions during squally weather and cyclonic storms are rough to very rough over coastal and offshore sea areas.
- Fisherfolk already out at sea must return immediately to the nearest coast, harbor, or designated safe anchorage.
- Deep-sea fishing vessels must strictly avoid venturing into central and deep sea regions during active gale and storm warnings (wind speeds 65-85 kmph gusting to 95 kmph).

## Squally Weather and Deep Sea Safety Measures (Source: IMD Marine Fishermen Warning Bulletin, Page 1)
- All fishing vessels and motorized boats must carry operational marine communication gear: VHF/SSB radios, GPS navigators, and NAVTEX receivers.
- Maintain real-time contact with coastal fisheries stations and Coast Guard emergency frequencies.
- Every person on board must wear certified life jackets; inspect life-saving appliances, buoyant distress signals, and distress beacons (EPIRBs) before sailing.
- Anchor boats securely at coastal jetties, bays, or haul craft onto high ground above the maximum spring tide level during storm surge and cyclone alerts.

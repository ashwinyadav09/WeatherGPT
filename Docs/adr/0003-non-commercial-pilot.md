# 0003: Non-commercial pilot

**Status:** accepted (6 November 2026; the product owner confirmed the product is non-commercial)

**Context:** IMD says its APIs are free for non-commercial use only. Open-Meteo's free tier is non-commercial and limited to 10,000 calls a day.

**Decision:** Run WeatherGPT as a free, ad-free, public-interest pilot. Register for IMD API access stating that purpose. Attribute every source and stay within each limit.

**Consequences:** No payments, ads or resale of source data. Any monetisation reopens licensing before it ships. Costs stay low through caching and free or credit-backed tiers.
